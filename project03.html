Design Statement & Architectural Core Value

Responding directly to the spatial and safety diagnoses established in Project 02, Canyon Echo radically reconceives the arrival threshold and artistic core of Sierra Canyon School. Rejecting the conventional monolithic institutional box, the scheme deconstructs the school's initials (“S-C”) into a continuous, elevated pedestrian gateway ribbon that lifts arriving students entirely clear of driveway traffic.

Concurrently, the new Art Center is sculpted into faceted, stepped volumes that echo the surrounding canyon rock strata, housing open visual studios, subterranean performance auditoriums, and panoramic exhibition galleries. A three-dimensional hillside promenade bridges the academic building with the sports gymnasium, transforming once treacherous elevation changes into a vibrant social and artistic landscape. The proposal is substantiated through exhaustive orthographic drawings and meticulously crafted, illuminated physical scale models.
