Design Statement & Architectural Core Value

Situated within Nanjing Guochuang Park adjacent to the 600-year-old Ming Dynasty City Wall, this project investigates how lightweight, contemporary timber tectonics can engage with monumental historic masonry. Reinterpreting the rhythmic masonry arches and defensive perimeter of the ancient wall, the design introduces a radial glulam rib structure sheltered by a translucent tensile mesh roof and anchored by a central multi-media “Super Trend Box”.

Functioning as an adaptive civic apparatus, the pavilion effortlessly transitions across four distinct operational modes—everyday leisure, weekend creative market, corporate roadshow, and curated contemporary exhibition. By mediating between industrial heritage and historical relics, the proposal activates the civic commons while offering a porous, flexible threshold between history and modern public life.
