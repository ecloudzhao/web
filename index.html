ABOUT ME

Visual art and architectural design are how I make sense of the world and discover who I am. Through designing compositions, experimenting with different media, and developing spatial imagery, I find my voice and build confidence.

Beyond personal exploration, I believe art and architecture are powerful tools for raising social awareness and expanding understanding. Combining architectural morphology with material and archival research, my work investigates physical discipline, educational surveillance, and the emotional resonance of spatial boundaries.

Art and architecture are how I ask: What do we choose not to see? How can I make the invisible visible? Through creating, I challenge bias and foster the empathy necessary for genuine understanding.
